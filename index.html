⚙️ App Settings



📰 Full Screen News Ticker

Shows a small scrolling message at the bottom of every TV channel while the player is in Full Screen.

Choose how long one ticker scroll takes. The default is 5 minutes; 10 minutes is extra slow. Long messages are automatically slowed further for readability.

Use a direct HTTPS/HTTP image URL. Logo is displayed small and will not cover the video.

🤝 Full Screen Sponsored Logos

Displays a small sponsor photo box only while a TV player is in Full Screen. Each photo shows for 10 seconds, then the next photo slides in from the right. The box slides up from the bottom and hides after the list finishes. The sequence repeats every 10 minutes.

Or add logos one at a time using the Add Logo button. The URL is added to the list above and appears in Preview immediately; press Save Sponsored Logo Settings to publish it.

Paste direct image links only (for example, a URL ending in .png, .jpg, .jpeg, or .webp). Up to 20 photos. Delete a URL line to remove a photo; reorder lines to change display order. Each photo displays for 10 seconds and the sequence repeats every 10 minutes.
